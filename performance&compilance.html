Bring every purchase into a controlled sourcing process, before it becomes off-contract spend.

Intercept requisitions from your ERP or intake channel and route them through structured sourcing workflows. Enforce policy at the point of request with automated approvals, supplier selection rules, and competitive bidding requirements.

Every event is tracked, every decision is documented, and every award follows a defined process. Data flows back into your systems, giving you full visibility into compliant vs. unmanaged spend.

Your team enforces procurement policy by default—not after the fact.

nexPro applies enterprise-grade security controls and independent certifications (SOC 2, ISO 270001, GDPR) to protect procurement data as automation scales.

With audited governance, role-based access, and encrypted data ownership, You can expand digital and AI-driven sourcing with confidence.

Gain clear visibility into where demand enters, how sourcing capacity is deployed, which suppliers are engaged, and where savings and compliance are being realized—or left on the table.

Role-based dashboards connect buyer execution metrics with executive-level insights on savings, throughput, adoption, and supplier behavior.

Procurement can prioritize effort, remove bottlenecks, and demonstrate sustained value to Finance and the business.

Additional Features

Executive Overview

Enterprise visibility into savings, cycle time, sourcing throughput, and supplier performance—built for CPO and CFO reporting.

Compliance Embedded

NDAs, insurance limits, safety clauses, and policy rules auto-apply based on category, region, and spend threshold.

SOC 2 Certified

Audited controls across security, availability, and confidentiality ensure sourcing activity operates within repeatable, enterprise-grade safeguards.

ISO 27001 Certified

A formally governed information security management system reduces global risk exposure while supporting complex, multi-region sourcing operations.

Zero Data Retention

Customers control how AI is deployed—opting out, using open-source models, or leveraging enterprise LLMs with zero data retention and contractual privacy protections. Procurement data is never reused or trained outside customer control.

Governance at Scale

One platform to run all sourcing—from tail spend to complex RFx—using procurement-trained AI, adaptive workflows, and governed analytics.
