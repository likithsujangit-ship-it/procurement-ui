Intake Automation Capabilities: Requester AI Copilot, AI Event Builder, and RFx Templates

Request a Demo

KIT helps requesters tell you what they really need, the first time: scope, specifications, timelines, and optimal outcomes.

While chatting with KIT, requesters are asked natural follow-up questions to form complete requests.

KIT takes an approved ask and builds the right event around it, whether it’s a quick tail spend buy, a tactical quote, or a strategic multi-round RFx.

Stay focused on stakeholder alignment while KIT optimizes the structure, supplier approach, and scoring.

Standardize execution at scale.

RFx Templates draft the sourcing package for buyers: questions, pricing tables, requirements, and scoring criteria matched to your category and scenario.

Don’t start from scratch. Upload your pricing model, requirements matrices, evaluation model, and more spreadsheets, then adjust in the platform for reuse.

Additional Features

AI Request Assistance

Input what you need like you’d tell a coworker. The system translates it into a compliant request, then guides you through the next steps in minutes.

Automated Routing

Stop routing requests through inbox chains and handoffs. Direct each submission to the right team at the moment of intake.

SOW Builder

Prevent vague service requests from turning into scope gaps and renegotiations. Convert a few sentences into a structured SOW with deliverables, milestones, and pricing models.

Price Books + Punchouts

Let teams order from familiar supplier sites. nexPro stays the system of record. Punchouts keep the experience intact. Price books bring your catalogs in so KIT can surface the right item.

ERP/P2P Integration

nexPro integrates with your ERP and P2P systems so sourcing starts from real demand and flows back into requisitions, POs, and supplier records automatically.

Compliant at the Start

Eliminate follow-up chaos by capturing complete procurement requirements on the first pass. Adjust the form in real time as the request evolves, forcing the right inputs and removing guesswork.

Approval Workflows

Approvals shouldn’t be difficult. Set rules by spend, category, or department, and the right people get pulled in automatically.

End-to-End Visibility

You’ll always know the status of a request. Track progress from intake to award with a clear status that updates in real time.

Why Sourcing Leaders Love Intake

Scale Procurement Fast

Move more spend under management without adding headcount. Friendly AI Intake captures clean requirements up front, drafts compliant SOWs, and launches RFx in minutes. Teams are saving 500+ hours a month on SOW creation alone, while improving governance and reducing downstream risk.

Risk Management at the Start

Stop unmanaged spend and last-minute requests. Intake automatically classifies every request against your policies, routes it to the right workflow, and pushes contracted buys to catalogs. The right teams receive the right sourcing assignments. Less leakage, fewer workarounds, and a clean audit trail from day one.

Easy Adoption

First-time requesters get guided questions that capture clean details fast, while power users move even faster with reusable, policy-aligned templates. Supplier responses return in a structured and comparable format by default, so teams spend less time fixing data and more time making strategic award decisions.
